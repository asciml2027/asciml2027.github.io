## About the workshop

The **{{ site.data.conference.name }}** is planned for **{{ site.data.conference.dates_label }}** at **{{ site.data.conference.venue.name }}** in Fukuoka, Japan.

The workshop will provide a forum for research at the intersection of mathematics, scientific computing and machine learning. It aims to encourage discussion of mathematical foundations, computational methods and applications to scientific problems, and to support exchange among researchers working in these areas.
